### Best Outreach Angle

Lead with ownership, capacity planning, decision cadence, and cross-functional delivery. Keep the note focused on the operating challenge and the value Trisha can add, rather than restating the application.

### Who To Look For

- recruiter or talent acquisition partner supporting the function
- business operations leader

### What To Avoid

- Repeating the full application story.
- Claiming knowledge of people or priorities beyond the posted role.
- Contacting several people with identical notes.

### Suggested Timing

Use one targeted networking note before applying, ideally after the role and likely team have been reviewed. If there is no reply, apply on schedule rather than waiting, and send one concise update after submission if the contact is relevant.
