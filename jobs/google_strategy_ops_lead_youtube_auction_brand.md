# Strategy and Operations Lead, YouTube Auction Brand

Company: Google
Location: Los Angeles, CA
Salary range: US $152,000-$222,000 USD + 15% bonus target + equity + benefits
Official source: Google Careers
Official URL: https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/95974486699844294-strategy-and-operations-lead-youtube-auction-brand

## Role Summary
Google is hiring a Strategy and Operations Lead for YouTube Auction Brand within Go-to-Market Operations. The role supports Google's Global Business Organization and partners with Product Go-To-Market, Americas Large Customer Sales, and YouTube to drive activation strategies for YouTube Brand Auction products.

The role focuses on YouTube revenue growth, AI-powered campaign types, YouTube Brand Auction products, Reach and Frequency, YouTube Shorts, product activation, seller enablement, operational excellence, cross-functional alignment, and stakeholder influence across Americas Large Customer Sales.

## Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 6 years of experience in management consulting, sales operations, business strategy, or corporate advisory.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Understanding of the customer and market landscape, and how brands build on YouTube and competitors.
- Ability to influence and achieve alignment among senior stakeholders, including Directors, with engaging objectives.
- Ability to convey complex information verbally and in writing, establishing links between customer objectives and Google's business objectives.
- Ability to own complex projects and datasets, manage project scoping, and drive operational excellence.
- Ability to develop unified activation strategies that drive a regional business while allowing for nuance across international markets with distinct needs.

## Responsibilities
- Drive awareness, commercialization, and activation of YouTube Brand Auction products across AI-powered campaign types anchored to key Global Business Organization business objectives.
- Lead forums with sector-level video leads to ensure sector YouTube activation strategies are aligned to Americas Large Customer Sales Objectives and Key Results and Business Forecast Meetings.
- Track and report on progress toward annual goals, providing critical product updates and feedback programs.
- Activate training, events, and communications to educate Americas Large Customer Sales sellers on how to best activate new offerings with customers.
- Collaborate with cross-functional partners to drive future YouTube Brand Auction product development.
- Identify market-level feedback, product gaps, communication opportunities, adoption OKRs, and value propositions.
- Provide thought leadership on product activation and forward-looking product-related business opportunities.
