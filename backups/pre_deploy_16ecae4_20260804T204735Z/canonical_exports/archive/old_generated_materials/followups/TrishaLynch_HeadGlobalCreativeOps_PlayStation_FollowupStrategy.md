### What To Avoid

- Repeating the full application story.
- Claiming knowledge of people or priorities beyond the posted role.
- Contacting several people with identical notes.

Send the first targeted note 5 to 7 business days after the 2026-06-27 submission. If there is no reply, send one concise follow-up 7 to 10 business days later. Pause after that unless there is a meaningful update or a response.
