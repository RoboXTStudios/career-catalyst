## Description

Crunchyroll is looking for a Director, Enterprise Strategy & Initiatives to help shape and operationalize strategic programs across a global streaming entertainment organization. This role partners with executive leadership, business operations, content, marketing, product, finance, and regional teams to turn complex priorities into clear plans, aligned operating rhythms, and measurable outcomes.

The ideal candidate brings strong enterprise strategy, operational planning, executive communication, stakeholder alignment, and change management experience. This person should be comfortable working across streaming entertainment, fandom, and global teams while improving processes that help the business scale.

## Responsibilities

- Lead enterprise strategy workstreams that connect company priorities to operational plans, milestones, and executive decision-making.
- Drive cross-functional initiatives across business operations, marketing, content, product, finance, and regional teams.
- Build clear operating models, planning cadences, and process improvement recommendations for strategic programs.
- Prepare executive communication materials that synthesize risks, dependencies, tradeoffs, and progress updates.
- Partner with stakeholders to improve alignment, remove blockers, and support change management across global teams.
- Translate ambiguous business needs into structured plans, owners, timelines, and measurable outcomes.
- Support streaming entertainment and fandom-focused initiatives that require coordination across markets and functions.

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in enterprise strategy, business operations, strategic programs, management consulting, or related operational leadership roles.
- Proven ability to lead cross-functional initiatives in complex, fast-moving organizations.
- Strong executive communication skills, including written updates, presentations, and decision-ready recommendations.
- Experience with operational planning, process improvement, stakeholder alignment, and change management.
- Comfort working with global teams across multiple functions, regions, and levels of seniority.
- Entertainment, streaming, gaming, fandom, or consumer technology experience preferred.
- Ability to balance strategic thinking with practical execution and follow-through.
