# Director, Marketing Operations

Company: Paramount
Location: Studio City, CA
Work arrangement: On-site
Salary range: $130,000-$180,000 USD + bonus eligible
Official source: Paramount Careers
Official URL: https://careers.paramount.com/job/Studio-City-Director%2C-Marketing-Operations-CA-91604/1354305300/

## Role Summary
Paramount Television Marketing is hiring a Director, Marketing Operations. Marketing Operations is the connective layer between Marketing Strategy and Creative Production. The role acts as the central hub for creative resource management across a multi-brand marketing organization, ensuring creative assignments are prioritized, resourced, and routed efficiently across CBS, MTV, BET, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central, and Paramount+.

The role owns visibility and orchestration of creative capacity, partners closely with Campaign Managers and Creative leadership, balances workload, anticipates bottlenecks, and supports informed assignment strategy. The ideal candidate is a systems thinker, relationship builder, and calm force within a fast-moving environment who brings clarity to complexity and enables creative teams to do their best work.

## Responsibilities
- Lead the centralized resourcing model for creative teams, ensuring optimal talent-to-task alignment.
- Maintain real-time visibility into capacity, demand, and workload across AV, Design, Motion, Art, and related creative groups.
- Act as the decision-making partner for prioritization and sequencing of assignments.
- Anticipate high-volume cycles and proactively identify needs for external support.
- Filter and balance incoming work to maintain sustainable prioritization and protect focus for strategic initiatives.
- Design and maintain streamlined processes for intake, prioritization, assignment, and delivery.
- Establish consistent end-to-end processes that support both rapid-turn daily programming and longer-lead 360 campaigns.
- Ensure cross-team alignment across Strategy, Campaign Management, and Creative.
- Improve visibility into project flow for leadership to make informed decisions.
- Simplify handoffs between teams and create standard operating processes that scale.
- Own and optimize operational tools that support marketing workflows, including Airtable, AI, Jira, SharePoint, and more.
- Partner with technology and production operations teams to streamline systems supporting campaign planning, project flow, resourcing, reporting, and file management.
- Lead AI enablement across Marketing Operations to increase speed, reduce repetition, and support creative teams.
- Build dashboards for leadership visibility into throughput, capacity, and performance.
- Serve as the central point of connection between Marketing Ops and creative department leadership.
- Cultivate strong collaborative relationships across brands and creative groups to align priorities, timelines, and expectations.
- Provide ongoing clarity and communication around what is active, what is upcoming, and where bottlenecks exist.
- Elevate operational insights to Marketing leadership so they can deploy resources, adjust priorities, and mitigate risks proactively.

## Basic Qualifications
- 7+ years of experience in Marketing Operations, Creative Operations, Production Management, or a related function within entertainment, broadcast, or creative agency environments.
- Experience developing, monitoring, and reconciling project budgets to support operational and creative decision-making.

## Additional Qualifications
- Head of Production or Senior Project Management experience at an entertainment company or advertising/promotion agency preferred.
- Experience supporting multi-brand or matrixed organizations.
- Familiarity with broadcast cycles, content pipelines, and 360 campaign launches.
- Strong experience with workflow systems, operational tools, or marketing project management platforms.
- Strong analytical skills and ability to synthesize insights into operational recommendations.
- Exceptional communication and influencing skills.
- Comfort with ambiguity and ability to bring order to complex systems.
- Experience implementing AI tools or workflow automation is a plus.
