# Director, Ad Operations & Technology

Company: Sony Interactive Entertainment / PlayStation
Location: San Mateo, CA hybrid. Fully remote may be considered.
Salary range: $224,400-$336,600 USD
Official source: PlayStation Careers

## Role Summary
Sony Interactive Entertainment is seeking a Director of Ad Technology & Advertising Operations to lead the strategy and execution of its global advertising technology and operations ecosystem. The role will shape the future of PlayStation's ad tech approach, drive programmatic and customer-focused monetization, and build a scalable global organization within a gaming and entertainment environment.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define and implement the global ad tech roadmap across ad serving, programmatic monetization, audience targeting, measurement, and privacy-first data activation.
- Lead build versus buy decisions, vendor selection, and platform integrations with Product and Engineering.
- Drive scalable, automated, audience-based advertising solutions.
- Own global advertising operations, including campaign execution, trafficking, yield management, forecasting, and optimization.
- Establish scalable processes, tools, and performance standards across regions, formats, and demand sources.
- Partner with Sales and Commercial teams while maintaining operational excellence and advertiser performance.
- Partner with data and analytics teams on ethical, measurable, future-proof audience strategies.
- Enable advanced reporting, insights, and experimentation for monetization and product decisions.
- Build, lead, and scale a distributed global organization across the US, UK, and Japan.
- Set team charters, development paths, performance expectations, and accountability.
- Partner with Product, Engineering, Sales, Legal, Privacy, Finance, ad tech vendors, platform partners, and industry forums.

## Qualifications
- 15+ years of experience in ad technology, ad operations, or digital monetization.
- Deep understanding of programmatic advertising, ad tech platforms, and yield optimization.
- Experience owning and evolving complex ad tech stacks.
- Experience leading global, distributed teams and managing managers.
- Strong critical thinking with hands-on operational execution.
- Experience in gaming, entertainment, streaming, or premium digital platforms preferred.
- Familiarity with global data privacy and advertising regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
