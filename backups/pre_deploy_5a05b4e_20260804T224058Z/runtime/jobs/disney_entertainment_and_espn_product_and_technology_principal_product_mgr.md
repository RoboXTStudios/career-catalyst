Job Summary:
The Content Platforms & Operations Asset and Media Services Team is seeking a
Principal Product Manager
to drive the evolution of products and operational capabilities that power the acquisition, inventory management, transformation, and distribution of The Walt Disney Company’s world-class content through the internal media supply chain.
This role is ideal for a product leader who thrives at the intersection of product strategy, platform operations, and execution. You will own and influence a portfolio of interconnected capabilities that span multiple systems, teams, and business functions, ensuring that operational needs, product investments, and long-term platform strategies remain aligned. Rather than focusing on a single product area, you will partner across the Asset and Media Services ecosystem to identify opportunities, define priorities, and deliver solutions that improve operational efficiency, scalability, reliability, and business outcomes.
As a strategic bridge between business stakeholders, operations teams, engineering, and design, you will translate complex operational challenges into product investments and roadmap priorities. You will lead product initiatives from discovery through delivery, coordinating across multiple product teams, technical dependencies, and implementation milestones to ensure successful execution and adoption.
Success in this role requires the ability to navigate ambiguity, balance competing priorities, and drive alignment across a broad stakeholder landscape. The ideal candidate brings strong product judgment, operational expertise, systems thinking, and a proven ability to deliver outcomes in complex, highly dependent environments. You will continuously evaluate evolving business needs, industry trends, and emerging technologies to shape the future of Disney's content supply chain while ensuring the operational excellence required to support content distribution at global scale.
Responsibilities and Duties of the Role:
Define and execute product strategies, roadmaps, and priorities that support the evolution of Disney’s content supply chain while delivering measurable business and operational value.
Partner closely with business, operations, engineering, architecture, and design teams to identify opportunities, solve complex problems, and deliver scalable product capabilities across Asset and Media Services capabilities and workflows.
Lead product initiatives from discovery through delivery, coordinating across multiple teams, stakeholder groups, and technical dependencies to drive successful outcomes.
Drive product strategy and operational readiness for emerging content workflows, new business capabilities, and evolving technologies, ensuring successful adoption and integration within the broader media supply chain.
Translate business and operational needs into clear product requirements, investment priorities, and actionable roadmaps that balance customer needs, business objectives, and technical feasibility.
Drive stakeholder alignment and influence strategic decision-making across a portfolio of products, operational workflows, and platform capabilities.
Establish product goals, success metrics, and operational KPIs, using data and user insights to measure outcomes and inform future investment decisions.
Champion product management best practices, including product discovery, roadmap planning, backlog prioritization, experimentation, and iterative delivery.
Evaluate emerging technologies, industry trends, and evolving business needs to identify opportunities for innovation, operational efficiency, and continuous improvement.
Develop business cases and cost-benefit analyses to support product investments, strategic initiatives, and operational transformation efforts.
Mentor and support the growth of team members while fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, innovation, and continuous learning.
