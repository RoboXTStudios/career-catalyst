Best Outreach Angle

Lead with creative and marketing priorities, workflow, quality, capacity, and dependable delivery. Keep the note focused on the operating challenge and the value Trisha can add, rather than restating the application.

Who To Look For

- recruiter or talent acquisition partner supporting the function
- creative or marketing operations leader
- relevant cross-functional operations leader
- former partner or professional contact who knows Trisha's work

What To Avoid

- Repeating the full application story.
- Claiming knowledge of people or priorities beyond the posted role.
- Contacting several people with identical notes.

Suggested Timing

Use one targeted networking note before applying, ideally after the role and likely team have been reviewed. If there is no reply, apply on schedule rather than waiting, and send one concise update after submission if the contact is relevant.

Core Value Point

I bring operational structure to creative and marketing work while protecting the momentum teams need to execute.
