# Head of Global Creative and Product Development Operations

Company: Sony Interactive Entertainment / PlayStation
Location: United States, Remote / San Mateo, CA collaboration
Salary range: $184,100-$276,100 USD
Official source: PlayStation / Greenhouse
Job family: Licensed Merchandise, Global Creative Operations, Product Development Operations

## Role Summary
Sony Interactive Entertainment is seeking an experienced Head of Global Creative and Product Development Operations for the Licensed Merchandise Team. The role leads global creative and product development operations across licensed merchandise, creative assets, product development processes, style guides, tools, milestones, and cross-functional execution.

The role requires global operations, creative leadership, licensing experience, process design, deadline management, stakeholder influence, and the ability to bring the global vision, strategy, and operational plan to life across internal and external partners.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage the global creative and product development team.
- Lead creative and product development processes across the Licensed Merchandise Team.
- Coordinate milestones to successfully go to market.
- Partner with licensing leads, licensees, gaming studios, franchise teams, retail marketing, and cross-functional teams.
- Work with executives and internal and external stakeholders to deliver business results.
- Lead the full concept-to-production lifecycle for creative assets, ensuring teams deliver on time, on budget, and at scale.
- Design and implement streamlined workflows to improve speed, consistency, and quality.
- Lead planning and development of style guides, creative assets, product development tools, and the creation process.
- Collaborate with external agencies to source talent and manage creative project execution.
- Ensure alignment with brand standards and business objectives.
- Guide creative collaboration throughout the project lifecycle.
- Lead product development across categories for licensing deals and collaborations.
- Support branding, packaging, promotional and marketing collateral, trade show needs, and sales materials.

## Qualifications
- 15-20 years of experience in a creative leadership role within licensing and consumer products.
- Project management, design, marketing, or related background.
- Proficiency in project management tools.
- Strong project management abilities, including prioritization, deadlines, and resource management.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to collaborate across departments and influence stakeholders at all levels.
- Strategic mindset with understanding of market trends, consumer behavior, and brand positioning.
- Gaming or entertainment experience preferred.
- Experience managing studios in the creation of product development preferred.
