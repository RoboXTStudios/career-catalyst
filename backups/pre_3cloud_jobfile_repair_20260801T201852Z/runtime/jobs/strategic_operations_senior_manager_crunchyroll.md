## Job Description

Office of the COO and serve as a trusted partner to the VP, Strategic Operations. In this highly visible role, you'll help shape strategic priorities, lead planning and operating rhythms, and drive cross-functional initiatives that improve execution across Crunchyroll's global media and direct-to-consumer business.

You'll work closely with executive leadership to translate strategy into action, developing executive communications, building decision frameworks, and creating the governance, insights, and accountability needed to keep the COO organization aligned on its highest priorities.

Core Areas of Responsibility
Drive strategic planning and roadmap development across the COO organization
Lead the COO organization's OKR process, planning cadence, and operating rhythms
Build executive-ready presentations, business cases, and decision frameworks that support leadership discussions
Develop dashboards, reporting, and governance mechanisms that improve visibility into priorities, risks, and execution
Lead high-priority, cross-functional initiatives from planning through execution
Identify risks, dependencies, and opportunities, driving alignment and actions to accelerate progress
Partner with Finance and People to support resource, workforce, and budget planning
Represent the Office of the COO in cross-functional forums, driving alignment and accountability across teams
Partner closely with the VP, Strategic Operations on evolving priorities, executive communications, and special projects
We are considering applicants available to work a hybrid schedule, with three days each week onsite at our Los Angeles office.

About You
We get excited about candidates, like you, because...

8+ years of experience in management consulting and/or strategic operations, corporate strategy, or business transformation, with experience in media, streaming, entertainment, or direct-to-consumer subscription businesses
Strong executive communication skills with experience developing C-suite presentations, business cases, and decision frameworks
Experience leading strategic planning, OKRs, quarterly business reviews, and executive operating cadences
Exceptional analytical and financial acumen, with the ability to translate data into actionable recommendations and influence decision-making
A structured approach to problem-solving, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and drive alignment across cross-functional teams
MBA preferred, or equivalent experience in strategy, consulting, or operational leadership roles
