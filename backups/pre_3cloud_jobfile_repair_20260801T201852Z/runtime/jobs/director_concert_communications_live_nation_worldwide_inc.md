THE JOB The Director of Communications, Concerts will lead the development and execution of strategic communications initiatives supporting Live Nation Concerts’ touring business at both the national and local level. This role is responsible for driving impactful media strategies: building buzz, telling better stories, and making sure the press and fans feel the magic behind the shows. As a key member of the communications team, this individual will oversee integrated publicity campaigns across Live Nation’s roster of touring artists, partnering cross-functionally with internal teams, artist management, promoters, marketers, and external media partners to deliver compelling storytelling and high-impact earned media opportunities throughout the tour lifecycle. The Director of Communications, Concerts will play a critical role in shaping narratives, identifying cultural and media opportunities, and executing innovative communications strategies that amplify Live Nation’s touring business. This role reports to the VP, Global Touring Communications for Live Nation Concerts.
