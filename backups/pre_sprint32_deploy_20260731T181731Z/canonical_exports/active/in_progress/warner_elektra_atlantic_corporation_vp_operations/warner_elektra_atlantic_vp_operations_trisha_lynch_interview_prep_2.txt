# Interview Prep

## VP, Operations at Warner-Elektra-Atlantic

### Hiring Manager Lens

- Likely first question: What experience best prepares you to solve this problem: Run the operating processes that keep the business coordinated?
- Likely functional probe: The hiring manager is likely to probe Is this a formal people-management role, and what team or disciplines would report to it?.
- Likely concern: The hiring manager may hesitate if there is shared vocabulary without evidence of substantive ownership.
- Response strategy: Use specific decisions, actions, and results that map to the hiring manager's stated and inferred priorities.

### Likely Themes

- cross-functional
- music
- warner
- operations
- wmg
- global

### Proof Points to Prepare

- Designed scalable workflows, governance, implementation standards, handoff processes, and operational systems.
- Translated technical platform requirements into operational guidance for marketing, analytics, technology, creative, media, and leadership stakeholders.
- Led Supervisors, Senior Campaign Managers, Campaign Managers, and Coordinators responsible for campaign execution and platform implementation.
- Led enterprise campaign operations supporting Disney Studios Theatrical and Disney Streaming/DSS while coordinating creative, media, analytics, technology, and operations teams.
- Led operational readiness around campaign measurement, tracking quality, reporting alignment, and launch validation.
- Developed QA standards, launch readiness, implementation validation, campaign review processes, and operational quality controls.

### Story Prompts

- A time you turned an unclear cross-functional problem into a practical operating plan.
- A time you improved a process without losing the trust or judgment of the people doing the work.
- A time you used data, workflow design, or reporting to improve visibility and execution.
- A decision where you balanced speed, quality, stakeholder needs, and limited capacity.

### Requirement Gaps

- Missing from resume language: #LI-Onsite Salary Range: $150,000 - $200,000 Annually Salary ranges are included for job postings where required by law.
- Missing from resume language: We work at the intersection of creativity and commerce, partnering with artists, managers, and label teams to build thoughtful, high-quality products and experiences that fans care about, and marketing them to drive revenue and add value to our clients business.
- Evidence is currently absent: This position requires a minimum of 4 days per week in the office.
- Evidence is currently absent: We ignite passions and build connections across our diverse community of artists, songwriters, partners, and fans.
- Evidence is currently absent: Everything in music starts with a leap into the unknown, and we’re committed to keeping the faith, acting with integrity, and delivering on our promises.
- Evidence is currently absent: We value collaboration, ownership, and curiosity, and we’re building a forward-thinking team that’s excited to evolve how artists reach and engage their audiences.
- Evidence is currently absent: Strong financial and operational forecasting background, with experience partnering closely with finance on budgeting, margins, and inventory planning.

### Questions to Ask

- Is this a formal people-management role, and what team or disciplines would report to it?
- How much direct customer or client interaction and escalation ownership is expected?

### Voice Reminder

Be specific and conversational. Lead with the situation and decision, then the operating change and measurable result. Do not overstate company familiarity.
