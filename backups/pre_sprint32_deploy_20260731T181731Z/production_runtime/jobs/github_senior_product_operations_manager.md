## Job Description

About GitHub
GitHub is the world’s leading platform for agentic software development — powered by Copilot to build, scale, and deliver secure software. Over 180 million developers, including more than 90% of the Fortune 100 companies, use GitHub to collaborate, and more than 77,000 organisations have adopted GitHub Copilot.
Locations
In this role you can work from Remote, United States
Overview
GitHub and Microsoft's developer AI portfolio, spanning GitHub, Azure AI Foundry, and VS Code, is one of the highest-velocity product areas at Microsoft, shipping the tools and platforms that define how the world builds with AI. The Insights & Product Operations team drives operational excellence across this portfolio, serving as the connective tissue between Product, Engineering, Marketing, Revenue, and Support to make the full launch lifecycle predictable, scalable, and market-ready. We don't just run processes. We build automated systems that make the entire product and Go-to-Market (GTM) machine faster and smarter.
Responsibilities
We are looking for a Senior Product Operations Manager who is equal parts operator and systems thinker. You will drive release operations across our developer AI portfolio, running the rhythms that keep releases on track and driving cross-functional coordination between product, GTM, and engineering teams.
You will also own the strategy and backlog for AI-powered operational systems we're building to make Engineering, Product, Design and GTM teams dramatically more efficient. This is a role for someone who is deeply curious about how product and GTM teams actually work, where friction lives, and why. You understand the landscape of modern AI capabilities well enough to see where they can transform operational workflows at scale. You think in systems, you obsess over the right problems before jumping to solutions, and you know how to translate operational insight into a prioritized roadmap that delivers compounding value over time.
What You'll Do
Release Operations & Launch Readiness
Run the release readiness process across the portfolio, driving the operational rhythms and reviews that keep launches on track
Drive cross-functional coordination across product, engineering, PMM, and revenue teams, ensuring the right stakeholders are aligned at the right time on launch decisions
Define and monitor operational health metrics; use release data, customer signals, and stakeholder input to surface risks, inform decisions, and prioritize improvements
Calibrate communications for your audience, whether that's an engineering status update or an executive-facing readiness report, keeping stakeholders informed with clarity and consistency
AI-Powered Operational Systems
Drive the automation roadmap for the systems that make EPD and GTM teams faster: AI-powered release tracking, intelligent triage, self-service launch tooling, automated stakeholder reporting, and more
Deeply understand how product and GTM teams work today, identify the highest-value opportunities for automation, and define what to build next
Maintain and prioritize the backlog for operational automation, working with automation engineers to translate operational insight into shipped solutions
Define success metrics for the systems you own, measure adoption and impact, and iterate based on what you learn
Stay deeply fluent in modern AI capabilities (LLMs, agents, copilots, automation platforms) so you can identify where they unlock step-function improvements and where they don't
